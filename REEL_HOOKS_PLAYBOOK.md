# TrueYards Reel Hook and Virality Playbook

Every reel gets checked against this before render and again before publish. If a rule is broken, rewrite before shipping.

## 1. What Instagram actually rewards (ranking signals)

- **Watch time** is signal one. Instagram measures it on every reel.
- **Sends per reach** (DM shares) is signal two. It weighs most for reaching non-followers, and Instagram now leans on sends more than on comments.
- **Likes per reach** is signal three. It matters more for reach among followers.
- **Skip rate** is the share of viewers who swipe away in the first 3 seconds. A high skip rate kills distribution before anything else is measured.
- **Originality.** Reposted or aggregated content gets no recommendations. Original edits get pushed.
- **Length.** 30 to 60 seconds gets the best reach rate. Longer works only if retention holds.

So one reel has three jobs, in order:
1. Survive second 3.
2. Hold to the end.
3. Be worth sending to someone.

## 2. Why the first 3 seconds are everything

- Of people who watch 3 seconds, about 65% reach 10 seconds and about 45% reach 30 seconds (Meta data). Lose them at second 2 and the rest of the edit never gets seen.
- No movement in the first second gives about 23% retention. Camera plus subject movement gives about 52%.
- About 40% or more of viewers watch with the sound off. The hook has to work silent, which means the text is on screen in frame 0.
- Never open on a logo, a black screen, a fade-in, a "hi guys", or context-setting. Start in the middle of the action.

## 3. The hook stack (0 to 3 s)

A hook is three layers that land together:

| Layer | Rule | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| Visual | A real image that shows the tension, already moving (push-in, whip, slam, split). Frame 0 must make sense paused. | 0.0 s |
| On-screen text | 3 to 6 words, huge, high contrast, centre-top. States the conflict or the number. | 0.0 s, fully readable by 0.3 s |
| Spoken line | 5 to 10 words. The conflict lands by 1.5 s. No intro words ("so", "today", "did you know"). | starts at 0.0 to 0.2 s |

Then there's a **pattern interrupt at about 1.5 to 2.5 s**: a cut, a reveal, a number change, or a beat drop. It gives a second reason to stay right when the thumb is deciding.

### Frame-0 test (mandatory)
Pause the reel at frame 0 and ask:
1. Without sound, do I know what the tension is?
2. Is there something real (a place, a number) I want explained?
3. Is it visually different from the last 3 things in a feed?

All three must be yes.

## 4. Hook archetypes that work for property and money

Visual rule for every archetype (Rishi, 2026-11-17): pair the hook line with an impossible-but-real hero image (a skyscraper in the middle of Lutyens Delhi; the twin towers between two real buildings). Hyper-realism beats animation and diagrams. See STYLE_SYSTEM.md "Hero shot rule".

Pick one per reel. Rotate them across reels.

1. **Contradiction.** Two true facts that seem impossible together. "India's capital. Almost no skyscrapers."
2. **Number slam.** A shocking number on screen in frame 0. "₹50,000 → ₹88 crore."
3. **Visual contrast or split.** The same distance or price, with two worlds side by side (Delhi flat vs Gurgaon towers).
4. **Forbidden or hidden.** "This is illegal to build in Delhi." "The rule nobody tells you."
5. **Stakes for the viewer.** "If you're buying in Noida, watch this before you pay."
6. **Countdown or list promise.** "3 reasons Delhi can't build up." This sets an open loop with a visible end.
7. **Myth bust.** "Delhi doesn't lack money. It lacks permission."
8. **Time jump.** "In 1931 this was planned. In 2026 the rule changed."

What makes a hook weak:
- a question the viewer can answer instantly
- a topic statement with no tension ("Let's talk about Delhi's skyline")
- a slow zoom on a nice but generic photo
- text that appears after 0.5 s

## 5. Story structure after the hook

**Hook (0 to 3 s) → Open loop (3 to 6 s) → Escalating beats → Twist or payoff → Loop ending**

- **Open loops** come from curiosity, which is an information gap (Loewenstein). Name the gap early ("3 reasons", "and the third one surprised me") and close it late. Nest a smaller loop inside the big one.
- **Escalate.** Each beat should be bigger or stranger than the last. Save the best fact for the last third.
- **Always pay off.** If the hook promises something and the reel doesn't deliver it, people stop trusting you and stop sending.
- **Don't signal the end.** No "so that's it", no outro card that tells people to leave.

## 6. Pacing and retention

- Change something visual every 1.5 to 3 s: a cut, a zoom, a text change, an overlay or a number.
- One idea per screen. Keep on-screen text to 7 words or fewer per line.
- Words reveal at the voice's timestamps, so the text never leads or lags the voice.
- Voice uses short phrases with natural pauses capped at about 0.4 s. There's never dead air longer than 0.6 s.
- **One colour theme per reel**: white plus one accent (red or green) for every highlight, tag, ruler and end card. Never mix accents.
- **Music works with the edit**: use a steady-BPM beat track (ElevenLabs Music, BPM stated in the prompt). Detect beats, offset the track so a downbeat lands on the hook's key word or visual event, and snap scene cuts to the nearest beat (within 0.2 s). Music sits about 9 dB under the voice and comes forward between lines.
- **Mix image types for the job, never one type for a whole reel.** Real photos only where the actual look of a landmark is the point (proof shots). photoreal AI images with designed overlays for hooks; clean motion graphics for comparisons, maps, rules and numbers (towers rising, height lines, laser cuts, stamps). AI images for mood, history and the future. Label AI and 3D visuals.
- **Look: realistic and professional, like a news documentary.** Use photoreal AI images or real photos, a slow camera push, clean lower-third text, thin news-style callouts and one decisive event (an erase, a stamp, a scan). No cartoon or game-style 3D (Rishi rejected it).
- **Hooks are built, not found.** A stock photo with text over it is not a hook. Frame 0 needs a designed motion-graphic event (something growing, slicing, slamming, counting) that tells the story without sound.
- **Text must read on any frame.** Never put coloured text straight on a bright or hazy photo. Use a solid or blurred backing panel, a dark scrim of at least 60% behind text, or white text with green as a highlight block, not green letters. Check a frame mid-reveal, not only finished frames.

## 7. Share triggers (why someone hits send)

Based on STEPPS (Berger) and the NYT sharing study (Berger and Milkman):
- **High-arousal emotion** gets shared: awe, surprise, anger at an unfair rule, anxiety about money. Sadness and calm get the least sharing.
- **Social currency.** "I knew this before you." A non-obvious fact about a city people live in.
- **Practical value.** Something that changes a buying or renting decision.
- **Triggers.** Tie it to something people see daily (the metro, their rent, the airport).
- **Identity and local pride.** Delhi vs Gurgaon vs Noida debates make people tag friends.

End with a **send prompt that's a question with a side to pick**: "Would you live on the 50th floor in Delhi?" or "Send this to your friend who still says Gurgaon is better."

## 8. Loop endings

- The last frame should flow back into frame 0, using the same image or completing the opening sentence. Rewatches count as watch time.
- Give the question or CTA in the last 2 s, then cut. No 3-second branded tail.

## 9. Testing (Trial Reels)

- Use Trial Reels (shown to non-followers only for about 72 h) to A/B test two hooks on the same body.
- Change only the first 3 seconds between variants.
- The winner is the variant with the lower skip rate and higher sends per reach, not the one with more likes.
- Log results in `drafts/<reel>/results.md` and promote the winning hook pattern in this file.

## 10. Pre-publish QA checklist

- [ ] Frame 0 passes the paused, sound-off test
- [ ] Text is visible at 0.0 s, 6 words or fewer, inside the safe zone (top 250 px and bottom 400 px kept clear of UI)
- [ ] Spoken conflict lands by 1.5 s
- [ ] Pattern interrupt between 1.5 and 2.5 s
- [ ] Visual change every 3 s or less
- [ ] Open loop named in the first 6 s, closed near the end
- [ ] Imagery mixed by purpose (real for proof, 3D/motion for the hook and data, AI for mood); AI and 3D labelled, photos credited
- [ ] Every text block passes contrast on its worst frame (backing panel or scrim; no green letters on light photos)
- [ ] Every number checked against a source, with sources in fine print or the caption
- [ ] Ends on a pick-a-side question or send prompt, loops cleanly
- [ ] 30 to 60 s total
- [ ] Education only, no buy/sell calls, 5 hashtags or fewer, no em dashes
- [ ] Visual style is different from the last reel
