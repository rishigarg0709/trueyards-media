## Verified facts (checked 2026-11-17)
1. On 23 July 2019 the Supreme Court cancelled Amrapali Group's RERA registration, cancelled its land leases with the Noida and Greater Noida authorities, appointed a court receiver and appointed NBCC to complete the pending projects; petitions concerned over 42,000 home buyers (Tribune, 24 Jul 2019; ThePrint, 24 Jul 2019).
2. The court found home buyers' money had been diverted (forensic audit: dummy companies, bogus bills, undervalued flat sales) (ThePrint, 24 Jul 2019; Tribune).
3. As of Oct 2026, NBCC's Phase-I scope is about 37,250 units, about 35,000 complete and about 23,700 handed over; Phase-II (about 8,200 units) expected in 2028. On 23 Oct 2026 handovers began at Adarsh Awas Yojana, Greater Noida West, the last Phase-I project where they had not started (Indian Masterminds, 24 Oct 2026).
4. Buyers were paying rent and home-loan EMIs together while waiting (Indian Masterminds, 24 Oct 2026, describes this).
5. RERA (2016) Section 4(2)(l)(D): promoters must keep 70% of the amounts received from buyers in a separate bank account for that project, withdrawn only in proportion to completion (Conventus Law summary of the Act).
Dropped: total amount diverted (sources give partial figures only, e.g. ₹837 crore in bogus bills; no single verified total); "49,000 homebuyers" (headline only, body unsupported); exact years of waiting (projects vary).
Wording rules: "about", "around"; no names of individuals; no blame beyond what the court said; no advice to buy or not buy.
