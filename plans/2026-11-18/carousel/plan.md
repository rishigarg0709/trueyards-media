## Verified facts (checked 2026-11-17)
1. Project scope 1,445 km including spurs (Gadkari, Rajya Sabha, March 2026), commonly described as about 1,350 km for the main corridor (Business Today, 5 Nov 2026; Wikipedia).
2. 929 km (about 64%) operational as of 25 Mar 2026; the figure predates later openings (Business Today, 5 Nov 2026). Always say "as of March 2026".
3. Opened in stages: Sohna to Dausa Feb 2023; Madhya Pradesh section Sep 2023; Vadodara to Bharuch Feb 2024; Kota to Laban Dec 2024; Bharuch to Kharel Jan 2026; Vadodara to Godhra (trial) Apr 2026; Limkheda to Godhra (about 55 km) Oct 2026 (Business Today, 5 Nov 2026).
4. Foundation stones laid March 2019 (Wikipedia, Delhi to Mumbai Expressway).
5. Original completion target 2024; the Delhi to Vadodara section missed its June 2026 target; package 26 (about 30 km, Limkheda to the MP border) expected Dec 2026 or Jan 2027; the three southern packages near Maharashtra revised to March 2028 (Business Today, 5 Nov 2026).
6. Sanctioned cost ₹96,547 crore; ₹77,558 crore (about 80%) spent by Dec 2025 (Business Today, 5 Nov 2026).
7. Promised travel time Delhi to Mumbai: about 24 hours down to about 12 hours once complete (Business Today; Wikipedia).
8. 8 lanes, expandable to 12 (Wikipedia).
Dropped: state-wise lengths (Wikipedia only, and they sum to the 1,350 km figure, not 1,445); wildlife crossings and tree counts (single source); any land price or "growth corridor" claims (not education-only).
