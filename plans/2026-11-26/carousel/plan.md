## Verified facts
1. 75.7% of Singapore's resident population lived in HDB flats at 31 Mar 2026 (HDB Key Statistics FY2025). SingStat: 77.2% of resident households lived in HDB dwellings in 2025. VO says "about three in four residents".
2. Home ownership rate of resident households: 91.2% in 2025 (SingStat General Household Survey 2025). VO "nine in ten households own their home".
3. HDB residential flats under management: 1,167,093 at 31 Mar 2026, of which 62,672 rental (HDB Key Statistics FY2025). VO "over eleven lakh flats".
4. HDB was formed in February 1960, replacing the Singapore Improvement Trust; its first five-year programme ended in 1965 with 54,430 units built; sales under a home ownership scheme began in 1964; from 1968 CPF savings could be used to buy HDB flats (NLB Infopedia; gov.sg explainer, updated 24 Nov 2026).
5. HDB flats are sold on 99-year leases (HDB Key Statistics lists flats booked on "99-year lease, short lease, or remaining lease"); when the lease runs out the flat is returned to the state (PM Lee, National Day Rally 2018, MND extract).
6. FY2025 BTO 4-room selling prices before grants, Sengkang: S$344,000 to S$472,000 (HDB Key Statistics FY2025). At about ₹72 per S$ (Wise mid-market, Nov 2026, approx.) that is about ₹2.5 crore to ₹3.4 crore. VO: "from about two and a half crore rupees, before grants". Fine print: "S$ converted at about ₹72, Nov 2026, approx."
