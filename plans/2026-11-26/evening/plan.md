## Verified facts
1. Dharavi covers just over 2.39 sq km (Free Press Journal, 30 May 2025; Oneindia, 30 May 2025). Dharavi Notified Area 251.24 ha, of which 108.99 ha is for redevelopment (Business Standard, 9 Jun 2025).
2. Population estimates range from about 3 lakh to nearly 10 lakh people; about 60,000 families (Oneindia); about 56,971 households documented as of 19 May 2025 (Business Standard). VO "up to ten lakh people" and "about sixty thousand families".
3. Master plan approved at a review meeting chaired by the Chief Minister in late May 2025 (27 or 28 May, sources differ; VO says "May 2025"): 58,532 residential plus 13,468 commercial and industrial units, about 72,000 in all (Oneindia; Business Standard).
4. Ground-floor structures built on or before 1 January 2000 get 350 sq ft homes within Dharavi (Business Standard; Business Today, 13 Jul 2026); 2000 to 2011 residents get a Dharavi home after paying ₹2.5 lakh (Business Today). "Free" for pre-2000 families: Business Standard describes 350 sq ft as 17% more than other SRA projects; plan caption says "at no cost under the scheme".
5. About 95 million sq ft of rehab housing must be built before about 130 million sq ft of saleable space (Business Today, 13 Jul 2026).
6. Phase 1: 10,000 rehabilitation homes within about 18 months (Chief Minister in the Assembly, Business Today, 13 Jul 2026). Construction started on railway land in Matunga (Business Standard; Tribune/ANI 27 Dec 2025).
7. Operator: Navbharat Mega Developers Pvt Ltd, a joint venture of the Maharashtra government and a private developer (Business Today; Business Standard gives 80/20 private/state). VO says "a joint venture of the state and a private developer".
8. Bandra Kurla Complex lies next to Dharavi (geography; shown only in the AI visualisation hero, no claim spoken about it).
Dropped: "over a million" (FPJ figure, higher than the range others give); cost figures (sources disagree); rehab land outside Dharavi (acre figures conflict).
