## Verified facts
1. Delhi: male 6%, female 4%, joint male plus female 5% (Ujjivan Small Finance Bank, 13 Nov 2025; HomeFirst, 6 Aug 2026; Sobha, updated 12 Oct 2026). Registration 1% of deed value (HomeFirst, plus ₹100 pasting).
2. Haryana: urban 7% men, 5% women; rural 5% and 3% (Sobha, updated 12 Oct 2026). Only one source read in full tonight: the 17:13 run should open cleartax.in/s/stamp-duty-and-registration-charges-in-haryana to confirm before using the Haryana line; if it does not match, drop the Haryana line (the reel works on Delhi alone). Haryana registration is not 1% (slab fee), so the VO says "registration fees" without a percentage.
3. Mumbai: 6% men, 5% women, women get 1% off for a home in their sole name (Squareyards, updated 7 Jul 2026; Sobha). Squareyards says joint ownership with a male co-owner pays the male rate, while Sobha says the concession covers joint with spouse: they conflict, so the VO says only "in her own name".
4. Uttar Pradesh: 7% men, 6% women, with a concession for women up to ₹1 crore (HomeFirst, 11 Jun 2026; Sobha); not used in VO because the sources disagree on how the extra rebate stacks.
5. Money (our arithmetic, labelled example): ₹1 crore x 6% = ₹6 lakh; x 4% = ₹4 lakh; x 5% = ₹5 lakh; gap ₹2 lakh. Duty is charged on the higher of the deal value and the circle rate (HomeFirst).
Dropped: Ujjivan's "additional 1% transfer duty over ₹25 lakh in many zones" (no source given, not in the other two); NDMC zone rates.
