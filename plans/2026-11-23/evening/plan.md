## Verified facts
1. Height 1,428 ft (435 m); width no more than 60 ft (18 m) (HowStuffWorks; KSL, 18 Aug 2022).
2. Slenderness: "a width ratio of 24:1" (HowStuffWorks), "1 to 24" (Architectural Record, 4 May 2021), "1-to-23½" (KSL). 1,428 / 60 is about 23.8, so the VO says "about twenty-four times".
3. 84 floors, 60 apartments (HowStuffWorks; KSL).
4. Sway: as much as 3 ft (0.9 m) in high winds (HowStuffWorks); upper floors "sway around by a few feet" (KSL). VO: "up to about a metre".
5. Tuned mass damper "located above the highest occupiable floor" (Architectural Record); a counterbalance of "tuned steel plates" (KSL). Weight not given in the sources, so no weight in VO.
6. Two shear walls on the east and west give stiffness (Architectural Record); "About 200 rock anchors descend at most 100 feet into the underlying bedrock" (KSL).
7. Base: the landmark Steinway Hall, 1925 by Warren and Wetmore (Architectural Record; KSL says 1924), so VO says "from the 1920s".
8. Prices: $18 million to $66 million per unit (KSL, Aug 2022). At about ₹78.6 per US dollar (2022 average, approx.): about ₹141 crore to ₹519 crore. VO: "about 140 to 520 crore rupees".
9. Residents from April 2022; construction began 2013 (HowStuffWorks). Architect SHoP Architects.
10. India comparisons (our arithmetic): Qutub Minar is about 72.5 m, so 435 m is about six Qutub Minars; a cricket pitch is 22 yards (20.12 m) long, so the 18 m width is less than a pitch's length.
Dropped: concrete strength (unsourced); HowStuffWorks' $7.75 million studio price (conflicts with KSL); damper weight (no source).

## Caption draft
Steinway Tower: 435 m tall, only 18 m wide

New York's Steinway Tower is about 24 times taller than it is wide: as tall as six Qutub Minars, narrower than a cricket pitch is long. In a storm its top can sway up to about a metre, so a tuned mass damper near the top, heavy steel plates that swing against the wind, calms it. Two concrete spine walls and about 200 rock anchors in the bedrock hold it up, above a 1920s piano hall. Its 60 homes sold for about ₹140 crore to ₹520 crore each (2022).
