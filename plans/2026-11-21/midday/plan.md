## Verified facts (re-checked 20 Nov 2026 night)
1. About 110,000 subdivided units (SDUs) in residential buildings, about 220,000 residents (HK Government press release, 3 Oct 2025).
2. Median SDU floor area about 10 sq m (about 110 sq ft); about a quarter are under 8 sq m (AP, 14 Feb 2025).
3. A standard Hong Kong parking space is 12.5 sq m (about 135 sq ft) (AP, same).
4. Median SDU rent HK$5,000 a month (AP, same). Shown in rupees only: about ₹55,000 a month (approx., Feb 2025 rate of about ₹11.1 per HK$). Never say HK$ in VO, cards or caption (D14).
5. Residential land is 79 of 1,117 sq km, about 7% (Planning Department, Land Utilization in Hong Kong 2021).
6. Country parks and special areas: 44,312 hectares, about 40% of the land (AFCD Annual Report 2018).
7. Hong Kong about 75 lakh people (mid-2024, AP), about 6,700 per sq km (our calculation, approx.). Delhi (NCT): 1,67,87,941 people on 1,483 sq km, 11,320 per sq km (Census 2011). Fine print: "Delhi Census 2011; Hong Kong 2024, approx."
8. Basic Housing Units Ordinance in force 1 March 2026; minimum 8 sq m (about 86 sq ft), at least one window, ceiling at least 2.3 m (HK Government 3 Oct 2025; The Standard 17 Oct 2024; news.gov.hk 1 Mar 2026).
9. NEW tonight: by 28 Oct 2026 the Housing Bureau had registration applications for more than 75,000 SDUs, about 70% of the roughly 110,000; registration closes 28 Feb 2027; letting an unregistered unit becomes an offence from 1 March 2027 (HK Government, LCQ9 written reply, 28 Oct 2026).
