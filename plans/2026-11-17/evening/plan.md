## Verified facts (checked 2026-11-16)
1. Height 828 m (Arabian Business, "over 828 m").
2. Ground: about 4 m of loose silty marine sand a few metres down, then about 70 m of extremely weak to weak sandstone and cemented sand; groundwater about 2.5 m below ground level (TheConstructor case study, from the Poulos and Bunce foundation design).
3. Piles: 192 bored concrete piles, 1.5 m diameter, about 50 m deep (GeoPrac, citing Bunce of Hyder and Poulos of Coffey; TheConstructor lists 194, most sources 192, use 192). Load carried largely by skin friction along the shaft, boosted by shaft grouting (TheConstructor).
4. Raft: 3.7 m thick (GeoPrac, Arabian Business). Raft poured in four stages, 12,500 m³.
5. Foundations used about 45,000 m³ of concrete weighing more than 110,000 tonnes (Arabian Business).
6. Highly corrosive groundwater; high-density, low-permeability concrete and a cathodic protection system under the raft (Arabian Business). Cathodic protection works by an electric current that protects steel from corrosion.
Dropped: exact settlement numbers (sources differ on the limit).
