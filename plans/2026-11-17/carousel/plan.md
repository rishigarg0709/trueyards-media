## Verified facts (checked 2026-11-16; always say "check the state portal before you register")
1. Delhi: stamp duty men 6%, women 4%, joint 5%; registration fee 1% of the higher of deal value or circle rate, plus ₹100 pasting fee (NoBroker, updated 2 Nov 2026; Tata Capital confirms 6/4/5).
2. Haryana (urban, includes Gurugram): men 7%, women 5%, joint 6%; registration fee on slabs, capped at ₹50,000 (ClearTax Haryana guide).
3. Uttar Pradesh (includes Noida): stamp duty 7%; registration 1% (HomeFirst, updated June 2026). Women buyers get a 1% stamp duty rebate on property up to ₹1 crore, approved by the UP cabinet in July 2025 (earlier limit ₹10 lakh, max saving ₹10,000) (News On AIR, 23 Jul 2025).
4. Stamp duty is charged on the higher of the agreement value and the circle rate (NoBroker for Delhi; HomeFirst for UP).
Worked numbers for a ₹1 crore flat, assuming deal value equals circle value:
- Delhi: man ₹6 L + ₹1 L reg = ₹7 L; woman ₹4 L + ₹1 L = ₹5 L (₹2 L less)
- Gurugram: man ₹7 L + ₹0.5 L = ₹7.5 L; woman ₹5 L + ₹0.5 L = ₹5.5 L (₹2 L less)
- Noida: man ₹7 L + ₹1 L = ₹8 L; woman ₹6 L + ₹1 L = ₹7 L (₹1 L less)
- Delhi man vs Noida man: ₹7 L vs ₹8 L (₹1 L more)
Dropped: Mumbai and other states (sources conflict on the women's concession conditions); UP joint rate (single source).

## Caption draft
Stamp duty in Delhi, Gurugram and Noida: same ₹1 crore flat, very different bill.

Whose name is on the sale deed changes what you pay to register it.
Delhi: 6% for men, 4% for women, 5% joint, plus 1% registration.
Gurugram (Haryana urban): 7% men, 5% women, 6% joint, registration capped at ₹50,000.
Noida (UP): 7%, with a 1% rebate for women on property up to ₹1 crore, plus 1% registration.

Stamp duty is charged on the deal value or the circle rate, whichever is higher. Rates change, so check the state portal before you register. The person named becomes the legal owner.

Send this to whoever is deciding whose name goes on the flat.
