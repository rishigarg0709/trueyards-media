## Verified facts
1. US: the S&P Case-Shiller 10-City Composite fell 34% from its peak in April 2006 to its trough in January 2012; in 2014 it was still below the 2006 peak (Marquette Associates, Case-Shiller data).
2. China: new home prices fell 0.1% month on month in October 2026 and 2.4% year on year, the slowest annual fall since November 2025, in a years-long slump (Reuters calculations from National Bureau of Statistics data, via The Standard, 16 Nov 2026).
3. India: RBI All-India House Price Index rose 3.6% year on year in Q1 2026-27 (April to June 2026), index 117.5 (base 2022-23), 18 cities; quarter on quarter +1.1% (RBI press release, 24 Aug 2026).
4. India: CPI retail inflation was 4.38% in June 2026 (May 3.93%), new series base 2024 (MoSPI via Republic World, 13 Jul 2026).
Caveats: the HPI is a quarterly year-on-year figure and 4.38% is one month's CPI, so slide 6 says "roughly" and does not compute an exact real return; city-level HPI moves differ (Chandigarh, Jaipur, Kanpur, Lucknow, Thiruvananthapuram led the annual rise). The US figure is the 10-City index, not the national index.
Dropped: Japan land-price falls (sources unclear tonight, and the 17 Nov Japan reel covered it), Dubai 2008 (not verified), city-level India declines (RBI city table not readable tonight).
