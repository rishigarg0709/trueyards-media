## Verified facts
1. On 15 September 2006 the Delhi government notified mixed land use on 2,183 roads (Oneindia, 13 Apr 2007, describing the 15 Sep 2006 notification).
2. In April 2007 the Delhi government notified 355 more roads for mixed land use, in Rohini, Civil Lines, South and Central zones, after an MCD survey (Oneindia, 13 Apr 2007).
3. The Centre notified the Master Plan for Delhi 2021 late on 7 February 2007 (Oneindia and DNA, 8 Feb 2007, "notified late last night", Wednesday). MPD 2021 allowed mixed land use on 2,183 roads, commercial activity alongside homes (Oneindia, 8 Feb 2007).
4. The plan permitted about ten categories of professionals to work from residential areas, besides small shops of daily needs and services, and listed uses such as nursing homes, pathology labs, banks, gyms and coaching or tuition centres (Oneindia, 8 Feb 2007).
5. The plan gave traders relief from the "Damocles' sword" of sealing (Oneindia and DNA, 8 Feb 2007); later amendments were also aimed at protecting traders from a sealing drive by a Supreme Court appointed committee (The Quint, 9 Mar 2018).
Not claimed: size limits, conversion charges, or the history of the shop-house form (no source read this run).
