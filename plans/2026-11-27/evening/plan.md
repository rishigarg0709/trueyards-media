## Verified facts
1. Maharashtra: a refuge area is mandatory at every 7th habitable floor after the first 24 m; for buildings over 30 m the first refuge comes at the 24 m mark or the first habitable floor, whichever is higher; minimum 3 m wide, 10 sq m for residential and 15 sq m for non-residential (Square Yards, Refuge area bylaws in Maharashtra and Noida, updated 22 Nov 2024).
2. Refuge area is free of FSI; area above 4% counts toward FSI (Square Yards). Piramal: it "must not exceed 4% of the habitable floor area it serves".
3. National Building Code 2016: refuge areas are mandatory in buildings over 24 m (Piramal Realty, 4 Jul 2025; Housing.com). Sources disagree on the exact NBC frequency, so the VO quotes the Maharashtra rule only.
4. A refuge area cannot be sold and cannot be converted into staff quarters, a kitchen or a society office; no tall walls or fencing; never locked with a key (Piramal Realty).
5. Why: the stair fills with smoke faster than people can descend (InfraLens, NBC 2016 refuge note); residents wait there until evacuation or firefighting is safe and it avoids a stampede on the stairs (Piramal Realty).
6. Some developers have turned refuge areas into flats (Housing.com). Not named in the VO.
