## Caption draft (Instagram)
Why does every Indian city have half-built towers that stand for years? 🏗️
Because most of them are not empty. In Anarock's count of completely stalled projects, about two thirds of the homes were already sold.
By May 2022, about 4.8 lakh homes in India's 7 biggest cities were stuck or badly delayed, about half of them in NCR. One Ghaziabad project on a 2022 list of stuck projects had 6,253 flats. The usual story: loans run for 3 to 4 years, projects take 7 to 8, the money runs out, then come cases and insolvency, and the buyer pays EMI and rent together. The government's SWAMIH fund (2019) had finished about 61,000 such homes by December 2025.
Send this to anyone paying EMI on a flat that isn't ready.
Why India looks like this #5. Follow for #6: one thing you see every day in India, explained, every day.
