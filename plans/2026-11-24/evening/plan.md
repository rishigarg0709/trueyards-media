## Verified facts
1. Residents installed grills after robberies and burglaries (Free Press Journal, 6 Apr 2022; The News Minute, 23 Sep 2021, quoting a resident via The Hindu).
2. Mumbai fire brigade chief Hemant Parab: keep grilled windows only if they are openable, with a lock that can be broken during evacuation; with box grills "people are trapped in the corner rooms" (FPJ, 6 Apr 2022). VO: "Mumbai's fire chief".
3. Firemen lose time breaking grills, and that delay "sometimes results in loss of lives" (Asian Age, 15 Oct 2018, on a BMC proposal for openable grills; adoption not confirmed, not used as a rule).
4. Bengaluru: a cylinder blast set fire to an apartment complex; two people died, trapped on a grilled balcony (TNM, 23 Sep 2021; fire on Tuesday 21 Sep). The BBMP circular of 22 Sep 2021 told residents not to install balcony grilles; modifying balconies after the OC and CC is "against the rules" and needs "pre-approval", citing the 2003 building guidelines and the National Building Code of India 2016 (TNM). VO says "without the city's approval". No names or ages of the victims anywhere.
5. Full grills without an openable section mean the flat may have to be cut open in a fire, raising risk for the rest of the building (Citizen Matters, 3 Sep 2020).
Dropped: monkeys, pigeons, child safety (no source tonight); NCRB fire-death figure (not verified).
