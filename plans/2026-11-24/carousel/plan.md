## Caption draft
They paid for their flats and still had to leave

In Tardeo, Mumbai, the top 17 floors of a 34-storey tower had no occupation certificate (OC). In July 2025 the Bombay High Court ordered them vacated, the Supreme Court refused relief, and residents left by 27 August. Keys and a possession letter do not make a flat legal to live in; the OC from the municipal corporation does. Mumbai has more than 25,000 buildings without one, and BMC bills such societies 50% extra for water. A new BMC amnesty (Sept 2026) aims to regularise about 20,000 of them.
