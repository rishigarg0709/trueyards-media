## Verified facts
1. Le Corbusier "had refused to have Sector 13"; "It is believed that this was largely because of a superstition that Europeans had around the number" (The Tribune, 11 Feb 2020). DNA (12 Feb 2020): he "considered number 13 as inauspicious and therefore, he did not include it". VO says "the popular story", not as proven fact.
2. Daily Pioneer (2020): "UT official records indicate" Sectors 12 and 13 were first demarcated as a cultural zone, later removed, and Sector 13 was left out for lack of "an appropriate area". VO: "official records tell a quieter story".
3. Sector 13 "never existed since its formation on November 1, 1966" (Pioneer); the UT Administrator approved the proposal and the notification was issued (Tribune, 11 Feb 2020): Manimajra became "Sector-13 (Manimajra)" (DNA). "After 54 years" (DNA headline); Pioneer says 53. VO: "more than fifty years".
4. Same notification renamed other areas: Sarangpur institutional area Sector 12 (West), Dhanas Sector 14 (West), Maloya and Dadu Majra Sector 39 (West), Industrial Area Phases 1 to 3 as Business and Industrial Park 1 to 3 (DNA). Used only in the caption, not VO.
5. Manimajra residents had asked for "Sector 13 or Sector 26 (East)"; the administration first planned "Sector M" (DNA). Used in caption.
6. Le Corbusier "divided it into 60 sectors" (Tribune 2020). Not in VO (unclear whether 60 counts the missing one).
Dropped: "India's first planned city" (contested); any sector size figure (not in the sources read tonight); Manimajra's age (no source tonight, VO only says "an old town").
