## Verified facts
1. Shani Shingnapur is a village in Ahmednagar (Ahilya Nagar) district, Newasa taluka, Maharashtra, about 35 km from Ahmednagar (Wikipedia, Shani Shingnapur).
2. Houses have door frames but no doors; none of the structures within about 1 km of the Shani temple have doors or locks (Wikipedia); people fix only door frames, no doors and no locks on lockers, because they believe the temple is a living abode of Lord Shani (Deccan Herald / IANS, 10 Jan 2011).
3. Some villagers put loose panels against door frames at night, only to keep out animals (Taipei Times, 7 Jan 2015).
4. UCO Bank opened a "lockless" branch there in January 2011 (inaugurated 6 Jan 2011): its front is a glass door with no lock (Taipei Times); "Our branch has doors, but they will never be locked" (Daily FT, 18 Jan 2011); cash is kept in a strongroom (Taipei Times); some of the six staff stay on the premises at all times (Deccan Herald).
5. First recorded robbery case: 25 Oct 2010, recorded by Sonai police, a visitor from Haryana lost valuables worth about Rs 35,000 (Deccan Herald, Jan 2011); the shrine trustee said it happened outside the village (Taipei Times).
6. By 2015 "a few" doors had appeared over the years (Taipei Times).
Dropped (conflicting or unverified): population (3,000 in 2011 vs about 5,000 in 2015), "zero crime ever", first police station year, any faith claim stated as fact.
