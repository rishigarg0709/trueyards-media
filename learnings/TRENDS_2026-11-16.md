## What wins (principles, never copy content)
1. News peg + "here is the money angle": financewithsharan turns this week's headline (Ramayana film, Nvidia CEO exit, US midterms) into a business/money explainer. Median 328K. TrueYards version: "this week's real estate headline, explained" (RBI rate call, metro approval, RERA order, a big land deal).
2. "What happens if..." hypotheticals: Dhruv's "What happens if $1 = Rs 100?" did 8.3M. TrueYards version: "What happens if home loan rates hit 6%?", "What if Delhi banned new cars?".
3. Practical "don't miss these" lists: labourlawadvisor's IRCTC hacks did 4.3M (80 s). TrueYards version: "5 checks before you pay a token amount", "registry day mistakes".
4. Voiceover beats trending music for explainers: 39 of 51 reels used original audio; music only for festival or entertainment posts. Keep voiceover-first; music stays a quiet bed.
5. Length: top explainers run 75 to 156 s, not 35 to 55 s. Our 35 to 55 s gate is too tight for story/explainer reels.
6. Real estate brands (Knight Frank, AssetYogi) get almost no reach: corporate, report-style posts do not travel. Our edge is creator-style storytelling, not brand updates.

## Changes adopted
- Add a weekly "headline, explained" reel slot (news peg).
- Add "What happens if..." to the hook library.
- Experiment: 75 to 90 s story reels vs 45 s, same topic family, judged on views and skip rate.
