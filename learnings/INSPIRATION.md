## 2026-11-16 | @financewithshashvat | carousel "Portfolio Diversification explained to a beginner" | instagram.com/p/DftptXAk0Mp
- Seen: about 8.8K likes, 258 comments, 229 shares in 2 days; about 13 slides. Clean cartoon illustrations on white, 2 to 3 short centred lines of text at the top of each slide, big illustration filling the bottom two thirds.
- Story: a whole finance concept told as one metaphor with a recurring character (a man with only one friend; if she leaves he has nobody; he makes friends from different backgrounds; then asset classes, correlation, "10 clones are not diversification"). Every slide is one beat of the story, so people swipe to see what happens.
- CTA: last slide pitches a free daily WhatsApp newsletter: comment "NEWS" and get the link by DM. Almost all 258 comments are "News", which feeds the comment signal and builds a DM list.
- Borrow:
  1. "Explained to a beginner" metaphor series: a real estate concept as a short human story (for example "RERA explained as a wedding caterer who took your advance", "Carpet vs super built-up as buying a pizza and paying for the box", "Home loan tenure as a gym membership that keeps renewing").
  2. A recurring ORIGINAL character (never a caricature of a real person; that post leans on a look-alike of a real CEO, which we must not do) so followers recognise the series.
  3. Layout: 2 to 3 short lines top, one big picture below, one beat per slide, 10 to 13 slides.
  4. Comment-keyword offer: a genuinely useful free resource (for example a RERA and documents checklist PDF) sent by DM when someone comments a word. Grey zone: Instagram demotes pure engagement bait, so only do it when the resource is real and useful, and not on every post.
- Conflicts to resolve with Rishi: he rejected cartoon or game-style 3D for reels; is a clean illustrated style acceptable for a carousel series?
- Status: proposed to Rishi.
