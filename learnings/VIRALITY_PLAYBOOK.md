# TrueYards virality playbook (from deep research, 2026-11-16)

Every reel, carousel and analytics run reads this. LEARNINGS.md (our own data) overrides it once a pattern is proven on our account.

## 1. What Instagram rewards (strongest evidence: Mosseri, Meta Reels system card)
- Reels go mostly to non-followers. Each gets a small test batch; it expands only if that batch beats predictions.
- Top signals: watch time, likes per reach, sends (DM shares) per reach. Sends matter most for non-followers.
- Meta predicts: watch under 3 s (skip), watching more than 95% of viewers of same-length reels, reshare, share off app, audio reuse, comment, follow.
- Completion is judged against reels of the same length. Longer is fine if it holds.
- Originality: no reposted footage, no other apps' watermarks, no other creators' clips. AI visuals are fine; label "AI visualisation"; never use a recurring photoreal AI person as host.
- Hashtags: max 5, descriptive only. Put the topic noun in caption line 1, on-screen title and voiceover.
- Reels over 3 minutes are not recommended to new people.
- 65% of reel views arrive in 3 days: judge posts at 72 h, flag breakouts at 2 h and 6 h.

## 2. Publish gate (every post must pass all)
1. SEND TEST: name who the viewer would send it to (a friend buying a flat, the family WhatsApp group, city friends). No answer = rewrite the angle.
2. Frame 0: motion or a striking visual, topic noun on screen, a specific number or gap. No logo intro, no slow fade. Must work muted (text) and with sound (voice).
3. Length 35 to 55 s by default. A mini-reveal every 5 to 8 s. Last line loops back to the first frame.
4. One target emotion: awe (marvels), anxiety then relief (scam, then how to check), amusement (weird facts), or indignation then explanation (rules). Never sadness-only.
5. On-screen text: one idea, 6 to 8 words per card, the keyword version of the voiceover, never a different sentence.
6. Every number has a source in the caption or last frame; estimates are labelled.
7. No engagement bait ("comment YES", "tag 3 friends", "share if you agree"), no buy/sell/invest advice, no builder or project promotion, no politics.

## 3. What to make (Rishi, 2026-11-16: no fixed topic quotas)
- Anything interesting about real estate, told as a story: facts, history, cities, marvels, money, rules, builders, weird facts, myth vs fact, global comparisons, news. Mostly India, some global.
- Pick by how strong the story is, not by category. The test: is there a surprising question, a turn, and a payoff someone would want to send? (The Delhi skyscrapers reel is the model: a familiar place, a puzzle, a reveal.)
- Story shape: familiar anchor, a specific gap, rising clues, a twist that beats expectations, payoff, loop back.
- Keep variety so we learn; do not post the same kind of topic three times in a row. Let our own data, not preset percentages, shift the mix over time.
- Language experiment: English text with Hinglish voice vs English voice; decide from our data.

## 4. Hook library (rotate; log the archetype in log.md)
1 Contradiction: "This 2BHK costs more than a villa. Legally."
2 Number slam: "Rs 1.6 lakh per sq ft. In India."
3 Forbidden: "Builders rarely explain this line in your agreement."
4 Stakes or loss: "Miss this document and you may never get possession."
5 Myth bust: "Myth: carpet area is what you pay for."
6 Time jump: "In 1970 this Mumbai flat cost Rs 40,000. Today?"
7 Direct question: "Why are Indian flats sold by super built-up area?"
8 POV: "POV: your builder is not RERA registered."
9 Doing it wrong: "You are comparing flat prices the wrong way."
10 Visual shock: "This city is sinking 25 cm a year."
11 Before and after: "Same street. 1990 vs 2026."
12 Ranking: "5 most expensive streets in India. Number 1 is not in Mumbai."
13 Quiz: "Guess the price before I reveal it."
14 Story cold open: "1911. The King announces a new capital. There is no city yet."
15 Unfinished loop: "This rule exists because of one fire..." (ending loops to start)
16 Versus: "Rs 1 crore in Mumbai vs Dubai vs Tokyo."
17 Hidden in plain sight: "Every Indian building has this. Nobody knows why."
18 Insider words: "OC, CC, Khata: 3 words that decide if your flat is legal."
19 Price-tag reveal: "What Rs 50 lakh buys in 6 Indian cities."
20 Authority: "The RERA Act says builders must do this."
21 News: "New rule this month: what it means for flat buyers."
22 Narrator confession: "I used to think freehold just meant expensive."
23 Scale awe: "This building uses more steel than the Eiffel Tower."
24 Why does X look like this: "Why are Mumbai buildings stained black at the top?"
25 Counterintuitive economics: "Why Japanese houses become worthless after 30 years."
26 Countdown: "You have 30 days to do this after buying a flat."

28 Mystery: "Why this 400-year-old stepwell never dries."

Formats to rotate: how X works, map zoom (space to street), timeline, versus split screen, top-N countdown, myth vs fact cards, guess the price, day in the life of a city, history mystery, why does X look like this, kinetic text (10 to 20 s), data race chart, builders and marvels profile, 3-part series ("Part 2" on screen).

## 5. Carousels (retention and saves, not reach)
- Add music to every carousel if the publishing route allows it (makes it eligible for video surfaces).
- Slide 1 hook, slide 2 a standalone second hook (Instagram reshows slide 2 to non-swipers), payoff before slide 6, summary slide last.
- Styles: checklist, glossary, data cards, myth vs fact, then vs now, map sequence, story.
- Judge on saves per reach and follows, not raw reach.

## 6. Daily metrics and thresholds (per post at 2 h, 6 h, 24 h, 72 h; organic only, boosted tagged separately)
- Sends per reach (shares/reach): under 0.3% weak, 0.5 to 1% solid, over 1% good, over 2% breakout.
- Saves per reach: over 1% good, over 3% excellent.
- Likes per reach: 3 to 5% healthy, under 2% weak.
- Hold ratio (avg watch / length): over 40% good, over 60% excellent; compare within length bands.
- Skip rate (reels_skip_rate, confirmed available): beat our 14-day median (small-account average is about 65%).
- Rewatch proxy: views/reach over 1.1.
- Follows per 1,000 reach: 2 to 5 typical, over 8 strong. (Reels do not return follows via API; use account follower change on the day.)
- Hook diagnosis: low hold plus low views/reach = hook failed. High hold but low sends = payoff not share-worthy.
- These benchmarks are weak industry heuristics; our own rolling medians are the real bar.

Breakout rule: at 6 h, reach at least 3x the 14-day median for that format and age, sends per reach at least 1%, hold at or above median. Then: reply to every comment, share to Stories, make it the boost candidate, and queue a follow-up (Part 2 or a deeper carousel) within 24 to 48 h. Never re-upload the same file.
Winner remix: a post in our top 10% for sends gets 2 to 3 derivatives within 2 weeks (same topic new hook; same hook new topic; carousel deep-dive). Change at least the hook, first 3 s and voice.

## 7. Experiment plan
- Weeks 1 to 4, hook tournament: fixed format (motion-graphic explainer, 35 to 50 s). Rotate 8 archetypes: number slam, contradiction, stakes, myth bust, story cold open, visual shock, versus, why does X look like this. Primary: hold ratio and skip; secondary: sends.
- Weeks 5 to 8: topic x geography x language. Primary: sends per reach and follows per 1,000 reach.
- Week 9 on: 70% proven winners, 20% adjacent variations, 10% wild experiments. Retire an archetype after 4 weeks in the bottom quartile. Re-test winners every 6 to 8 weeks.
- Trust a pattern only after 8 or more posts and 3,000 or more cumulative reach per arm (or 70% wins in paired Trial Reel twins). Watch-time differences settle after 5 to 6 pairs.
- Trial Reels (non-followers only, available in the publishing API): once eligible, post twin cuts with the same body and two different first 3 seconds.

## 8. Boosts
Only boost organic winners (top 10% sends and hold). Measure on cost per follow. Keep boosted posts out of organic learning data.
