# Platform voice: one creative, a different post on every platform

Rishi, 22 Nov 2026 14:52 (Directive D53): "post the content according to that platform's theme and audience. Creatives can be the same, but still post according to the platform's personalisation."

The video or carousel file can be the same everywhere. Everything around it is written fresh for each platform: the words, the opening line, the title, the hashtags, the question, the length. Never paste the Instagram caption anywhere else, and never just trim it.

Start every post from the 3-line claim ledger (the one claim, its proof and source, the caveat). Then write each platform's text from the ledger, not from another platform's text.

Standing rules still apply on every platform: education only, rupees with lakh and crore, no em dashes, no "no selling", "independent", "unbiased" or "no ads", no project names, prices, "enquire" or "DM us", AI visuals labelled, no buy or sell advice.

| Platform | Who is there, why they are scrolling | Voice | Shape of the post | Tags and links |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram (reel, trial first) | Young, mostly new to us, here for a quick surprising story | Curious friend. Short, punchy, a little playful | Line 1 is searchable and names the place or thing (it shows under the reel and in search). 2 to 4 short lines of the story, the source, then one line that gives a reason to follow | At most 5 hashtags, at the end. No links |
| Facebook (reel) | Older, families, home owners and small business owners, many from smaller cities. They share and comment more than they follow | Plain and warm, like explaining to a relative. No slang | Open with the everyday stake ("If you own a flat, this matters"). 3 to 5 full sentences. End with a question people can answer from their own life ("Has this happened in your city?") | 0 to 2 hashtags. No links |
| YouTube (Short) | People searching for an answer, and viewers who watch to the end | Clear teacher | Title: what a person would type in search, with the place or term, under 90 characters, no clickbait the video does not pay off. Description: 2 lines that answer the question, the source, then the trueyards.in and WhatsApp links (D30). Tags: 3 to 6 search terms | Links allowed and wanted here. A pinned comment with the links |
| Threads | People here for conversation and opinions | A person thinking aloud. First person plural is fine ("We looked into this") | One clear take in 2 to 3 lines, under 500 characters, ending with a real question that has two sides. It should read as a post even without the video | At most 1 hashtag. No links |
| X | News-minded, fast, sceptical | Dry and exact | The single sharpest fact with its number and source name, under 270 characters. One sentence of so-what. No question needed | No hashtags, no links |
| LinkedIn (company page) | Professionals: buyers with money to place, investors, developers, brokers, bankers | Analyst. Calm, specific, no hype, no emoji | Line 1: the decision or trade-off a buyer or investor faces. 3 to 5 short lines: the insight, why it matters for money or risk, the source. Close with a question a professional would answer from experience. Give the video a plain descriptive title | 0 to 3 hashtags at the end. No links in the post |

## Carousels
Instagram: the swipe story with music. LinkedIn: the same images work as a document-style post, with the analyst text above. Facebook: photos with the plain, warm text. Threads and X: the 2 to 4 strongest slides, not all of them, with the platform's own text.

## What may differ beyond the words
- Cover or first frame: the Instagram cover follows the house grid template; the YouTube title does the cover's job there.
- Cut: reel.mp4 for Instagram, Facebook, Threads and LinkedIn, reel-yt.mp4 for YouTube, reel-x.mp4 for X (already in the pipeline).
- Time of day: keep one publish time for now. The analytics run may propose a platform-specific time once a platform has 20 posts of its own data.

## How we learn what each platform likes
These audience notes are starting assumptions, not findings. The daily analytics run compares each platform's own numbers (op platform_stats) and writes what works per platform into learnings/LEARNINGS.md. When a platform's results contradict a row above, change the row and say so in log.md.

## Check before posting
Read the six texts side by side. If two of them share a sentence, rewrite one.
