The plot buyer's checklist
6 PAPERS
Before
you pay
for a plot.
01
Title deed
and its
chain
Proves the seller really owns it. Trace every sale, gift and inheritance back about 30 years.
RED FLAGA missing link anywhere in the chain.
02
Encumbrance
certificate
Shows registered loans and legal claims sitting on the land.
RED FLAGYou are only shown the seller's old copy. Pull a fresh one yourself.
03
Jamabandi
and
mutation
The revenue record should carry the seller's name and match the deed.
RED FLAGNames or areas that do not match.
04
Is it still
farmland?
Farmland sold as a "plot" is the classic trap. Ask for the land-use change or conversion order.
RED FLAG"The conversion is in process."
05
Layout approval
and RERA
Match your plot number to the approved layout. Then look the project up on your state RERA website.
RED FLAGNo licence or registration number in writing.
06
The ground
itself
Get the boundaries measured and marked before you register, not after.
RED FLAGThe seller points and says "roughly here."
