Why does Delhi have almost no skyscrapers? 🏙️

20 km away, Gurgaon and Noida are full of them. Delhi stayed low for three reasons:

1. Planes. Every building within 20 km of the airport needs a height clearance from the Airports Authority of India.
2. History. Delhi has 174 centrally protected monuments. Nothing new can be built within 100 m of them, and the next 200 m is regulated.
3. Lutyens' Delhi. The heart of the city is a bungalow zone, low-rise by design.

For years Delhi's tallest building was the Civic Centre, a government office at about 101 m. Noida's tallest, Cyberthum Tower A, is 213.7 m.

Then in August 2026, the Master Plan for Delhi 2047 removed height limits across most of the city, except areas like Lutyens' Delhi and near monuments.
