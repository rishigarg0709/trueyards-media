The clock you get at handover
5 years.
Report a defect within 5 years of taking the keys and the builder must fix it free within 30 days (RERA Act, Sec 14(3)).
Check 01 · OC
Ask for the OC.
The builder must get the occupancy certificate and share it (Sec 11(4)(b)). Once it is issued, you have 2 months to take possession (Sec 19(10)).
Check 02 · Carpet area
Measure every room.
Carpet area is the usable floor inside the walls: no external walls, shafts or balcony (Sec 2(k)). Compare it with your agreement.
Checks 03 + 04 · Water, walls, wiring
Find the damp.
Run every tap and drain. Check ceilings and walls for damp patches. Switch on every point and fan. Note each fault.
Checks 05 + 06 · Fittings, snag list
Every fault, in writing.
Match fittings to the spec list in your agreement. Send a dated snag list with photos. Hairline cracks and wear and tear usually don't count.
Check 07 · Conveyance deed
Get it in your name.
Ask when the conveyance deed will be registered: within 3 months of the OC unless a state law says otherwise (Sec 17(1)).
