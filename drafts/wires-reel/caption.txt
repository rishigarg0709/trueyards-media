Why does every Indian street have a tangle of wires? 🔌
A lot of it is not electricity. The power lines usually run at the top; much of the tangle below is cable TV, internet and optical fibre hung on electricity poles.
In July 2024 Bengaluru's power company, BESCOM, told operators to remove unauthorised optical fibre, data and dish cables from its poles by 8 July or it would remove them itself. Its survey counted about 2,335 km of illegal wires in one city. On one street they were back the next day.
Look up on your street tonight: how many of those wires are your neighbours' Wi-Fi?
