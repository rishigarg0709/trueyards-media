How Burj Khalifa stands on sand

The tallest building on Earth has no solid bedrock under it. Below Dubai is loose sand, then about 70 m of weak sandstone, with salty groundwater just 2.5 m down.

The fix: 192 concrete piles, each 1.5 m wide and about 50 m deep, holding mostly by friction. On top sits a 3.7 m thick raft. The foundations alone used about 110,000 tonnes of concrete, and an electric cathodic protection system guards the steel from the salt.

So is it standing on sand, or gripping it?
