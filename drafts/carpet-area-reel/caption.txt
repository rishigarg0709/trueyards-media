Carpet area vs super built-up area: your 1,200 sq ft flat is not 1,200 sq ft of floor

Brochures usually quote super built-up area: your carpet area plus your walls and a share of the lobby, lifts, stairs and clubhouse. That extra, called loading, is often around 30%. So a "1,200 sq ft" flat can have only about 920 sq ft of carpet. At ₹1 crore, that is about ₹8,300 per sq ft on paper, but about ₹10,800 per sq ft of floor you actually live on. Under RERA 2016, carpet area is the legal measure, so it is the first number to ask for.
