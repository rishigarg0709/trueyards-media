Carpet area vs super built-up area: your 1,200 sq ft flat is not 1,200 sq ft of floor

Brochures usually quote super built-up area: your carpet area plus your walls and a share of the lobby, lifts, stairs and clubhouse. That extra, called loading, is often around 30%.
