Noida twin towers: why India's Supreme Court ordered two towers taller than Qutub Minar to be blown up.

Supertech's Apex and Ceyane stood in Emerald Court, Sector 93A, Noida. Nobody ever lived in them.

The case in short:
2009: a revised plan added two towers on the green area shown to earlier buyers.
2012: another revision took them from 24 to 40 floors.
The gap to an existing tower was about 9 m. The rules required 16 m.
Residents were not asked for consent, as the UP Apartments Act requires.
2014: Allahabad High Court ordered demolition.
31 Aug 2021: the Supreme Court upheld it, found collusion by Noida's planning authority, and ordered refunds to buyers with 12% a year interest.
28 Aug 2022: about 3,700 kg of explosives brought both towers down in seconds.
