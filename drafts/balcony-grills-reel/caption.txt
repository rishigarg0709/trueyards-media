Why every Indian balcony has an iron grill, and why fire officers want it to open.

Most grills go up after burglaries in the area. But a fully closed grill can also trap you. In Bengaluru in September 2021, two people trapped on a grilled balcony died in an apartment fire, and the next day the civic body told residents not to add balcony grilles without its approval, citing building rules and the National Building Code.

Mumbai's fire chief asks for grills that open, with a lock you can break in an emergency.
