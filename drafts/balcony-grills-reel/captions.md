## Claim ledger
- Claim: a fully closed balcony or window grill that keeps burglars out can also trap the people inside during a fire; fire officers ask for grills that open with a breakable lock.
- Proof and source: Bengaluru, 21 Sep 2021, two people trapped on a grilled balcony died in an apartment fire, and BBMP's circular of 22 Sep 2021 told residents not to add balcony grilles without approval (The News Minute, 23 Sep 2021); Mumbai fire brigade chief on openable grills with a breakable lock (Free Press Journal, 6 Apr 2022); Citizen Matters (Sep 2020); Asian Age (Oct 2018).
