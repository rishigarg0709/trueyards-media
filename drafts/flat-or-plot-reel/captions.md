Title: Flat vs plot: what the undivided share of land means for a buyer

Flat or plot is usually argued on convenience. The land component is the part that gets missed.
A flat carries an undivided share of land (UDS): flat area over total built area, times the land. In Housing.com's worked example, 200 flats on 40,000 sq ft give a 1,000 sq ft flat about 228 sq ft of land.
Why it matters: the structure depreciates while land tends to hold or gain value (BankBazaar), and redevelopment entitlements are worked out from UDS. Brigade Group advises that the sale deed states it.
