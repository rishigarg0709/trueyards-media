Flat or plot? What a flat buyer really owns in the land

Every Indian family has this argument at least once. When you buy a flat, you get an undivided share of land (UDS): a slice of the plot the building stands on, shared with every other owner. In one worked example, 200 flats share 40,000 sq ft, so a 1,000 sq ft flat comes with about 228 sq ft of land.
Team plot: the whole land is yours. Team flat: security, lifts and a ready home.
If the building is ever redeveloped, your UDS matters, so check that the sale deed states it.
