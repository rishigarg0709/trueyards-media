Why Kerala has lakhs of locked, empty houses

The 2011 Census counted about 11.9 lakh vacant houses in Kerala, about one in ten, and over 3 lakh of them had four or more rooms. About 22 lakh Keralites live abroad, most in the Gulf, and in 2023 they sent home over ₹2 lakh crore. Much of that money builds a big house back home, while the next generation often moves away too. Pathanamthitta alone has over 61,000 closed homes, and in 2023 the state proposed a tax on locked houses, then dropped it within a month.
