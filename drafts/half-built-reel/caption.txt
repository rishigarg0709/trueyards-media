Why does every Indian city have half-built towers that stand for years? 🏗️
Many are not empty shells. In Anarock's count of completely stalled projects, about two thirds of the flats were already sold.
By May 2022 about 4.8 lakh homes in India's 7 biggest cities were stuck or badly delayed, half of them in NCR. One Ghaziabad project alone had 6,253 flats. The usual chain: a 3 to 4 year loan, a 7 to 8 year project, money runs out, then court cases and insolvency. The buyer pays EMI and rent together.
The SWAMIH fund (2019) had finished about 61,000 such homes by Dec 2025.
