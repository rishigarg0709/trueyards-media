# Why India looks like this #5: half-built towers (28 Nov 2026 midday)

## Claim ledger
- Claim: many of the concrete skeletons that stand for years in Indian cities are stalled housing projects, and most of their flats were already sold.
- Proof: Anarock: 220 completely stalled projects with 1.74 lakh homes in the top 7 cities, about 66% already sold (BloombergQuint); 4.8 lakh homes stuck or badly delayed by May 2022, about half in NCR (Anarock via PTI, Outlook Business, Jun 2022); one Ghaziabad project with 6,253 flats (Liases Foras via Business Standard, Sep 2022); SWAMIH fund (2019) about 61,000 homes delivered by Dec 2025 (Ministry of Finance, Jan 2026).
- Caveat: not every unfinished tower is stalled, some are simply slow; the counts cover the top 7 cities and older launches; education only, not advice.

That grey tower near your colony that has looked half-built for years may already belong to hundreds of families.

In projects that stopped completely, roughly two out of three flats had already been sold. Many of those buyers pay a home loan EMI and house rent every month while they wait.
