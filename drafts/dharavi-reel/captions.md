## Facebook
Mumbai, Dharavi, 2025: one of the most crowded places in India is being rebuilt, and the plan is meant to pay for itself.

Up to 10 lakh people live in about 2.39 square kilometres here, by most estimates. In May 2025 Maharashtra approved a master plan with 58,532 homes and 13,468 shops and workshops. Families who lived on the ground floor before 1 January 2000 and are found eligible get a 350 sq ft flat inside Dharavi at no cost; those who came between 2000 and 2011 pay ₹2.5 lakh. The money comes from the land: rehab homes are built first, and then the extra space is sold.
