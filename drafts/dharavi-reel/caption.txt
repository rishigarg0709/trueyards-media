Why does Mumbai look like this? Dharavi: up to 10 lakh people in about 2.39 sq km, right in the middle of the city.

The master plan approved in May 2025 has 58,532 homes plus 13,468 shops and workshops. Eligible families on the ground floor since before 1 January 2000 get a 350 sq ft flat inside Dharavi at no cost under the scheme; those from 2000 to 2011 pay ₹2.5 lakh. Rehab homes come first, then the extra space is sold to pay for it. Phase 1 promises 10,000 homes in about 18 months.
