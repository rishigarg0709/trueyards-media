Why so many Indian houses have steel rods sticking out of the roof

Those rods are the tops of the columns, left there on purpose. Many Indian homes are built floor by floor, as money comes in. Leave the rods out and the next floor's columns can lock into the old ones. Cut them off, and later someone has to drill into the columns and glue new bars in.

The catch: bare steel rusts, and rust can take up to six times the volume of the steel it eats. Rain running down the rods into the column can crack the concrete from the inside.
