Paying EMIs for a flat that never came? The Supreme Court stepped in twice on 19 Nov 2026.

Sonipat: buyers had a three-way (tripartite) loan deal with the builder and the bank. The project stalled and the builder went into insolvency. The court said there can be no coercive recovery from the buyers until the commercial court checks whether they really defaulted and whether the bank released the loan to the builder as the agreement said. In a three-way loan, the money is meant to go in stages as the building rises.

Bengaluru: about 4,500 buyers of a stalled project in insolvency, who had paid about 95% of the price, get no coercive measures till they receive their flats. The court also said such buyers should go to the High Court or NCLT first.
