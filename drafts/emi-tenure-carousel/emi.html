What changed
The repo rate went from 5.25% to 5.50%.
RBI's Monetary Policy Committee raised it on 7 October 2026 and moved its stance to "calibrated tightening".
Most new floating home loans are linked to an external benchmark like the repo rate. Your rate moves at the reset date in your loan agreement.

The quiet part
Same EMI means a longer loan.
When your rate rises but your EMI does not, the extra interest has to go somewhere. It goes to the end of your loan, as extra months.
Nothing changes on your bank SMS. It shows up in one line of your statement: EMIs remaining.

RBI rate reset rules for EMI loans
At a reset, you get a say.
01Choose a higher EMI, a longer tenure, or both.
02Prepay part or all of the loan at any time.
03A quarterly statement: principal, interest, EMIs left, annual rate.
04Tenure cannot be stretched so far that the loan grows.
05A fixed rate switch, if your lender's policy offers one.
