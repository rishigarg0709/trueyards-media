Why do Mumbai towers have an empty floor in the middle? 🏙️
It is a refuge area. In Maharashtra, towers above 24 metres need one on every seventh habitable floor. In a fire the stairs fill with smoke faster than people can get down, so residents walk down a few floors, step into open air and wait for rescue.
Up to 4% of the floor area it serves is free of FSI. It can never be sold, locked or turned into a flat or a society office.
If you live high up, find your refuge floor today.
