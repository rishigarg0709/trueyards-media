Mumbai was once rented out for £10 a year. 🏝️

In 1661, seven swampy islands called Bombay were part of a Portuguese princess's dowry when she married England's King Charles II. He found them too costly to run, so in 1668 he leased them to the East India Company for £10 a year, paid in gold.

In 1782 a governor defied his own bosses and started building the Hornby Vellard to join the islands. By 1845 the seven had become one.

One of those islands was Worli. In 2025, one square foot there sold for over ₹2.8 lakh.
