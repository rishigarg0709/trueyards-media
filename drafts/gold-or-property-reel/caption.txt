Gold or property? The numbers behind every Indian family's argument

My uncle says property never falls. My aunt says buy gold. Here are the numbers both of them skip.
Gold: about ₹18,500 per 10 g in 2010, about ₹1,36,950 on 16 Nov 2026. It also fell ₹1,690 in one weekend this month.
Property: RBI's house price index for 18 cities fell 0.6% in July to September 2025, and is up 3.6% a year in its latest reading.
A flat can earn rent or save yours. Gold earns nothing while it sits. Gold sells in minutes; a flat takes months, plus stamp duty.
