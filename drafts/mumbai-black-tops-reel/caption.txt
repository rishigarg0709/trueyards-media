Why are Mumbai's old buildings black at the top? 🏢
It is mostly not soot. It is alive.
Mumbai gets about 2.5 metres of rain a year, about 95% of it from June to September. Rain runs off the parapet and the window ledges, the plaster stays wet, and algae, cyanobacteria and fungi grow into a dark film. That is why the blackest lines sit right under the roof and the ledges.
So a plain repaint rarely lasts: clean the growth, seal the cracks, waterproof the parapet, then use an anti-algal coat.
