## Claim ledger
- Claim: possession keys do not make a flat legal to live in; the occupation certificate (OC) from the municipal corporation does. In Tardeo, Mumbai, the top 17 floors of a 34-storey tower had none and had to be vacated in 2025.
- Proof and source: Free Press Journal, 6 Sep 2025 (HC eviction order 15 Jul 2025, SC refused plea 1 Aug 2025, vacated 27 Aug 2025); Free Press Journal, 20 Oct 2025 (25,000+ Mumbai buildings without OCs); Zee Business, BMC data 2016-17 (societies without OC billed water at 50% extra).
- Caveat: tower, keys, office, meter and flat images are AI visualisation, not the actual building; family counts in reports conflict, so none is given; educational content, not legal advice.
