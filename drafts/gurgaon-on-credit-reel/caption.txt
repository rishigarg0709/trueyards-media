Gurgaon was bought on credit. 🧾

In the early 1980s, the land outside Delhi was dry farmland at about ₹50,000 an acre, as K.P. Singh of DLF later recalled. Banks would not lend for private land deals, so he went farm to farm. Most families owned 4 to 5 acres.

His trick: pay a farmer, borrow the money back, buy the next farm. Many farmers were paid only after the homes sold. DLF put together over 3,500 acres this way.

Then he brought GE to Gurgaon in 1997, and everyone followed. In 2026, 11.36 acres on Golf Course Extension Road sold for about ₹1,000 crore. Roughly ₹88 crore an acre.
