Why the houses in Shani Shingnapur have no front doors

In this Maharashtra village, about 35 km from Ahmednagar, homes have door frames but no doors, because families believe Lord Shani guards the village. At night some lean a loose plank across the frame, only to keep animals out.

In January 2011 UCO Bank opened a branch there with a glass front door that is never locked. The cash still sits in a strongroom. And the first theft case on record came in October 2010, when a visitor lost valuables worth about ₹35,000.
