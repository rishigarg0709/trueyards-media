## Facebook
If you have ever waited for a bus at Majestic in Bengaluru, you were standing where a lake used to be.

The Kempegowda Bus Station opened in 1969 on the bed of the old Dharmambudhi lake, which had been drained decades earlier. Many other lakes went the same way: one became a stadium, one a golf course, one a housing colony. Studies found the city's lake area fell by about 61 percent between 1973 and 2007, and experts say this is one reason some areas flood so quickly after heavy rain.
