Bengaluru's main bus station used to be a lake

Majestic, the Kempegowda Bus Station, opened in 1969 on the bed of the old Dharmambudhi lake. It is not alone: Sampangi lake became a stadium, Challaghatta a golf course, Koramangala lake a housing layout. Bengaluru had close to 261 lakes in 1961, and between 1973 and 2007 the lake area of Greater Bengaluru fell by about 61%.
