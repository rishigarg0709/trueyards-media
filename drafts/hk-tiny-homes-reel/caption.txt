Hong Kong homes smaller than a parking space

About 2.2 lakh people in Hong Kong live in subdivided flats: one old apartment cut into tiny rooms. The typical room is about 110 sq ft, smaller than a 135 sq ft parking space, and rents for about ₹55,000 a month. Yet only about 7% of Hong Kong's land has homes on it and about 40% is protected country park. Delhi packs in more people per sq km. Since March 2026 a new law sets a minimum of 8 sq m (about 86 sq ft) with a window, and about 70% of these flats had registered by October.
