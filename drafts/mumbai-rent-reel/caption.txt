The South Mumbai flat that rented for about ₹1,300 a month

In 2012 Bloomberg reported a family paying about ₹1,300 a month for a 2,600 sq ft flat on Nepean Sea Road, where market rent was over ₹1 lakh. The reason: Bombay's 1947 rent law froze rents at 1940 levels, and the 1999 Act allows only small yearly increases. Researchers found controlled rent can be one-thousandth of market rent. Landlords stop repairing, about 14,000 old cessed buildings age, and yet tenancies change hands for pagdi worth about 80% of ownership value. When that building was redeveloped, the tenants were paid about ₹14 crore to move. (Rupee figures converted from Bloomberg's figures at the 2012 rate.)
