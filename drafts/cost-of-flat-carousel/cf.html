01 + 02 · Delhi example
Stamp duty ₹6 lakh.
Registration ₹1 lakh.
6% in his name, 4% in hers, 5% jointly. Plus 1% to register.
03 · Flats still being built
GST ₹5 lakh.
5% on an under-construction flat, 1% if it counts as affordable. A ready flat with an occupancy certificate pays no GST.
04 · Often priced separately
Parking ₹1 to 5 lakh.
Broker 1 to 2%.
A parking slot can be a separate line on the bill. If a broker found the flat, the fee is usually 1 to 2% of the price.
05 · Before the keys
Maintenance, paid in advance.
Builders can collect up to 2 years of maintenance before handing over the keys. Ask for the figure in writing.
