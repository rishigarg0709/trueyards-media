## Facebook
If you have ever driven through Chandigarh, you may have passed Sector 12 and then Sector 14 without noticing what was missing.

For more than fifty years the city simply had no Sector 13. Many people say the planner, Le Corbusier, thought the number was unlucky. The UT's own records give a plainer reason: the land for 12 and 13 was first kept for a cultural zone, and 13 never got its own area. In February 2020 the old town of Manimajra was finally given the name Sector 13.
