Chandigarh went more than 50 years without a Sector 13

The sector numbers jump from 12 to 14. The popular story says Le Corbusier skipped 13 as an unlucky number. UT records point to a planning gap: the land for 12 and 13 was first marked as a cultural zone, and 13 never got a proper area.

In February 2020 the UT notified the old town of Manimajra as Sector 13. Residents had asked for 13 or 26 (East); the first idea was "Sector M".
