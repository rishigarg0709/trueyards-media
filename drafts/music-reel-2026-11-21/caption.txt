Why does this building start one floor up? It's a stilt floor.
Many Indian cities don't count it in FSI, so it becomes parking. Delhi allows four floors plus stilt.
The catch: in the 2001 Bhuj earthquake, 100+ open ground floor buildings fell in Ahmedabad, 225 km away.
Now the code asks for stilt columns designed for 2.5x the force.
