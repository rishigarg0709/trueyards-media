# Why Indian walls say "This property is NOT for sale": platform texts (D53)

## Claim ledger
1. Claim: the painted "not for sale" line is a warning to buyers, because empty homes get "sold" by people who do not own them.
2. Proof: Thiruvananthapuram, July 2025: an empty Jawahar Nagar house was sold for ₹1.5 crore while its owner lived in the US; a 76-year-old woman posed as the owner at the Sasthamangalam Sub-Registrar Office; it came out when the caretaker found the property tax already paid (Kerala Kaumudi, 9 Jul 2025). Unattended property is the usual target; keep mutation, tax receipts and the encumbrance record current (NRI Legal Services, Housing.com, 1acre.in).
3. Caveat: the paint itself is a signal, not protection; the reason any one owner paints it is not documented, so it is described as a warning sign. Scenes are AI reconstruction, not the actual house.
