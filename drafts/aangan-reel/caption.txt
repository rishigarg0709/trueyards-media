Why old Indian houses had a hole in the middle

The aangan, the open courtyard at the heart of many old Indian homes, was not just for family life. In a 250-year-old haveli in Bikaner, researchers logged 45°C outside on 21 June. The courtyard itself reached 43°C, but the bedroom beside it never went above 30°C, with no AC.

Three things did it:
1. Shade: a small, deep courtyard with a sunshade on top keeps the rooms around it in shade.
2. Thick walls: about 70 cm of wall delays the heat by about 10 hours, so it arrives at night when the air outside is cooler.
3. Airflow: the courtyard is the only way air gets in, so it keeps moving.
