Ahmedabad's pols: why some old house fronts were really escape routes

A pol is a gated lane of 50 to 100 houses sharing walls, inside the walled city Sultan Ahmad Shah I founded in 1411. Many took their fortress shape in the 1700s, when Mughals and Marathas fought over the city and its walls no longer kept robbers out.
Each pol had a heavy gate with a guard room above it, rooftops joined house to house, and narrow passages, some disguised as the fronts of homes, for escape in an attack.
In 2017 the old city became India's first UNESCO World Heritage City.
