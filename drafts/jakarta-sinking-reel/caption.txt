Jakarta is sinking, and the sea is not the main reason

Parts of North Jakarta have sunk up to 25 cm in a year, and some areas dropped about 2.5 m in 10 years. The main cause researchers name: groundwater. Homes and factories pump water from wells under their own floors, the soft clay below dries out and compacts, and the city drops with it. About 40% of Jakarta already sits below sea level, and coastal streets now live behind sea walls.

Indonesia's answer: build a new capital. Nusantara, in East Kalimantan on Borneo, more than 1,200 km away. Construction began in 2022. It is planned for about 1.2 million people by 2029, at an expected cost of about USD 45 billion. As of October 2026, about 10,000 people have moved in.
