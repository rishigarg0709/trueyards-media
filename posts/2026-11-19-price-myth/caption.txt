Property prices never fall? US home prices fell 34%.

The S&P Case-Shiller 10-City index fell 34% between April 2006 and January 2012, and in 2014 it was still below the peak. In China, new home prices were still falling in October 2026, down 2.4% in a year. In India, the RBI House Price Index rose 3.6% in the year to April to June 2026, while retail inflation was 4.38% in June. Prices can fall, stall or trail inflation, and every city moves differently. Check the index, not the brochure.
