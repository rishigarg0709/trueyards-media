Getting the keys to a new flat? 7 possession day checks to do before you sign the possession letter.

Under the RERA Act, you have 5 years from handover to report defects, and the builder must fix them free within 30 days (Sec 14(3)). So handover day matters:
1. Ask for the occupancy certificate (OC)
2. Measure the carpet area against your agreement
3. Run every tap and drain
4. Look for damp, switch on every point and fan
5. Match fittings to the spec list
6. Send a dated snag list with photos
7. Ask when the conveyance deed will be registered

Save this for handover day, and send it to the friend getting keys this year.
