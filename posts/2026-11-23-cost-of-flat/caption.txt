Cost of buying a flat in India: your ₹1 crore flat can cost ₹1.14 crore

The price on the brochure is not the price you pay. On a new ₹1 crore flat in Delhi:
Stamp duty: ₹6 lakh in a man's name (₹4 lakh in a woman's, ₹5 lakh jointly)
Registration: ₹1 lakh
GST: ₹5 lakh while the flat is under construction (none on a ready flat with an occupancy certificate)
Parking: often ₹1 to 5 lakh extra
Broker, if you use one: 1 to 2%
Maintenance: many builders take up to 2 years in advance

That is ₹1.14 to 1.19 crore before the deposit. Save this and ask for every line in writing before you book.
