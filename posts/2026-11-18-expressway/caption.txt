Delhi to Mumbai Expressway: promised by 2024, still being built in 2026

929 of 1,445 km was open as of March 2026, about 64%. It has opened one stretch at a time: Sohna to Dausa in Feb 2023, the MP section in Sep 2023, Vadodara to Bharuch in Feb 2024, Kota to Laban in Dec 2024, Bharuch to Kharel in Jan 2026 and Limkheda to Godhra in Oct 2026.

Delhi to Vadodara missed its June 2026 target. About 30 km near the MP border is now due Dec 2026 or Jan 2027, and the last packages near Maharashtra by March 2028. Sanctioned cost: ₹96,547 crore, of which ₹77,558 crore (about 80%) was spent by Dec 2025.

When a brochure says "minutes from the expressway", check which stretch is actually open.
