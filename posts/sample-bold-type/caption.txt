Is your flat legal? 3 words decide it: RERA, CC, OC.

RERA: the project is registered with your state's real estate regulator.
CC: the building was built as per the approved plan.
OC: the building is fit to live in.

Ask to see all three before you pay any token money.

Education only, not legal advice. Certificate names and steps vary by state. Source: Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016, and state building bye-laws.
