Only 6% of homes sold in India's top 7 cities now cost under ₹50 lakh. A year ago it was 10%.

JLL's January to September 2026 data shows where home buyers in Bengaluru, Mumbai, Pune, Delhi NCR, Hyderabad, Chennai and Kolkata actually went:

• Under ₹50 lakh: 12,689 homes, down 36%
• ₹50 lakh to ₹1 crore: 46,877 homes, down 18%
• ₹1 crore and above: 1,54,996 homes, up 23%, now 72% of all sales
• ₹1.5 to 3 crore is the single biggest band at 37%, up 54% in a year

Total homes sold rose 6%. The money spent on them rose 15%.

What this covers: new apartment sales in seven cities. It does not cover resale homes, plots or smaller cities.
