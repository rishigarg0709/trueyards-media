Six papers to see before a single rupee leaves your account for a plot.

1. Title deed and its chain, back about 30 years
2. A fresh encumbrance certificate
3. Jamabandi and mutation in the seller's name
4. Land-use change or conversion order
5. Approved layout and the RERA registration
6. Boundaries measured on the ground

Then have a property lawyer read all six. Record names differ by state.

Save this, and send it to whoever in your family is buying land.

General information, not legal advice.
