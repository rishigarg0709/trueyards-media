Home loan EMI didn't change after the RBI repo rate hike? Check your loan tenure.

RBI raised the repo rate from 5.25% to 5.50% on 7 October 2026. If your lender keeps your EMI the same at reset, the extra interest is added as extra months at the end of your loan.

Example: a ₹50 lakh home loan, 20 years, rate moves from 8.00% to 8.25%.
Keep the EMI: about 12 more EMIs, about ₹4.96 lakh extra interest.
Raise the EMI by ₹781: tenure unchanged, about ₹1.88 lakh extra interest.

RBI's rate reset rules for EMI loans let you choose a higher EMI, a longer tenure or both, prepay at any time, and get a quarterly statement showing EMIs left. Save the 10 minute check on slide 7 for before the December policy.
