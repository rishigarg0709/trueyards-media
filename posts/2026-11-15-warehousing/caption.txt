India leased 32.6 million sq ft of warehouse space between January and September 2026, the highest in three years (Vestian).

The interesting part is who took it. Logistics firms fell from 41% of leasing to 29% in one quarter, while manufacturing and auto moved up. Chennai led the quarter, ahead of Mumbai and Pune.
