Why does every Indian street have a shop downstairs? Delhi mixed land use, explained.
On many Delhi streets the shutter below and the family above came first, and the rule came later. On 15 September 2006 Delhi notified mixed land use on 2,183 roads in one go. 355 more roads followed in April 2007, and the Master Plan for Delhi 2021 (notified February 2007) allowed daily-needs shops, clinics, labs, banks, gyms and coaching centres in residential areas.
